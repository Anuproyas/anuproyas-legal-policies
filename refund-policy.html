Refund Policy

Effective date: 8 October 2026 · Please read carefully
Publication checklist: This is a practical draft for the Academy's current planned online learning services. Verify that it matches your actual practices, payment provider, course delivery and applicable law before publishing. It is not legal advice.

This policy applies to paid courses, subscriptions, digital resources and other paid Academy services unless a specific offer clearly states a different lawful condition before purchase.

1. Before purchasing

Please review the course description, format, access duration, device requirements, schedule and price before paying. Digital content and online services can have different refund treatment depending on whether access has started and applicable consumer law.

2. When a refund may be considered

We will review refund requests where a duplicate payment was made, payment succeeded but access was not provided and cannot reasonably be restored, a material service description was incorrect, the Academy cancels a paid service without a suitable replacement, or a refund is otherwise required by law. If a technical problem prevents access, contact us first so we can attempt to resolve it.

3. Digital content and started services

Except where applicable law requires otherwise or the Academy has failed to provide the promised service, fees for digital materials may be non-refundable after the resource has been accessed, downloaded or delivered, and course fees may be non-refundable after substantial course access or participation has begun. Any such restriction will be disclosed before payment and will not override mandatory statutory rights.

4. Cancellations and refunds by the Academy

If the Academy cancels a paid course or cannot provide the essential service, we will communicate the available remedy, which may include rescheduling, equivalent access or a refund for the undelivered portion, as appropriate under the offer and applicable law.

5. How to request a refund

Email helpdesk2026@yahoo.com with the purchaser’s name, order/payment reference, product or course, payment date, amount and reason. Do not send full card numbers, passwords or OTPs. We may request limited information needed to verify the transaction.

We aim to acknowledge requests within 7 working days and communicate the decision after reviewing the facts. Approved refunds will normally be sent to the original payment method; bank or payment-provider processing times may vary.

6. Chargebacks and suspected misuse

Please contact us first if you believe a charge is incorrect so we can investigate. We may provide relevant transaction and access records to a payment provider where necessary to respond to a dispute. We do not restrict any legal right to raise a genuine payment dispute.

7. Fair treatment and applicable law

Nothing in this policy removes consumer rights that cannot lawfully be waived. Where this policy conflicts with mandatory law, the law prevails. The Academy may update this policy for future purchases; the version shown at purchase should be retained for that transaction.
