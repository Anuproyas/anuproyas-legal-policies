Parents Consent

This page explains the consent and supervision expected when a parent or legal guardian enrolls or permits a child to use Academy services. It supplements, and does not replace, the Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.

1. Parent or guardian responsibility

Where a learner is below the age at which they can independently consent under applicable law, a parent or legal guardian should complete enrollment and provide any required permission. By submitting a consent or enrollment form, the adult confirms they are authorized to do so and that the information supplied is accurate.

2. Learning and communication

The parent/guardian understands that online learning may involve lessons, assignments, assessments, class announcements and communications through the platforms identified by the Academy. The parent/guardian should review age suitability and help the child follow class conduct and platform safety instructions.

3. Personal information

We seek only information reasonably needed to administer learning, communicate with the family, maintain safety and provide the requested service. Do not submit sensitive information unless specifically necessary and requested through an appropriate channel. See the Privacy Policy for details on use, retention, sharing and requests.

4. Online safety and recordings

Parents/guardians should supervise a child’s use as appropriate. If a class is recorded, the Academy should provide notice and explain the purpose, access and retention before recording. Do not record, photograph or share other learners’ images, voices or personal information without appropriate permission.

5. Withdrawal and contact

A parent/guardian may contact us to withdraw consent for future processing that relies on that consent. Withdrawal may affect features that require the information or permission, and does not make earlier lawful processing unlawful. Some records may need to be retained where legally required or reasonably necessary for security, dispute resolution or accounting. Email helpdesk2026@yahoo.com with the learner’s name and a way to verify parental authority; do not send identity documents unless we explain why they are needed.

6. Acknowledgement

By completing an enrollment or consent action, the parent/guardian confirms they have read the relevant policies, understand the nature of the online learning service, and consent to the processing and participation described there, subject to applicable law. Consent should be specific, informed and freely given; it should not be treated as blanket permission for unrelated uses.
