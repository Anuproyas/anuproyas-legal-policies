1. Introduction

Anuproyas Learning Academy (“we”, “our”, or “the Academy”) provides educational resources, study materials, learning opportunities, examination-related services, and related features through its website and associated platforms. This Privacy Policy describes information that may be collected, used, stored, and protected when you use those services.

2. Information we may collect

Depending on the features you use, information you submit may include:

  • Name and email address;
  • Class, course, or enrollment details;
  • Messages, questions, support requests, and submissions;
  • Information needed to operate an assessment or learning feature, where offered.

The website platform or service providers may also process technical information such as browser or device details, approximate usage information, and security logs.

3. How information may be used

Information may be used to provide educational resources and services; manage enrollment and communications; respond to questions; maintain, troubleshoot, and secure the website; improve learning materials; administer assessments where offered; and meet applicable legal obligations. We aim to use information for legitimate purposes connected with the service.

4. Children’s privacy

Our educational resources may be used by school-age learners. Where applicable law requires parent or guardian consent or additional safeguards for a child’s personal information, appropriate steps should be taken. Parents or guardians may contact us with privacy questions or concerns. Avoid posting unnecessary personal or sensitive information in public comments or general forms.

5. Sharing of information

We do not intend to sell personal information. Information may be processed by hosting, communication, classroom, assessment, or other service providers when needed to operate a feature. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users, rights, safety, or security. Third-party providers operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

6. Cookies and third-party services

Blogger and embedded or linked third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies and may process technical information under their own policies. If you follow an external link, use an embedded service, or access a third-party application, review that provider’s privacy policy. The Academy may not control third-party technologies.

7. Data security and retention

Reasonable and proportionate steps should be taken to protect information under our control. However, no website, online transmission, or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Information should be retained only as long as reasonably needed for its purpose, legitimate operational needs, or legal requirements.

8. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to ask about personal information you have submitted, request a correction, or raise a privacy concern. We may need to verify your identity or authority before acting. Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate recordkeeping.

9. External links

Our website may link to other websites, applications, or educational platforms. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party services. Review their policies before sharing personal information with them.

10. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when our services or practices change. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated effective or revision date. Please review this page periodically.
